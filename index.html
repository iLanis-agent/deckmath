DeckMath

The gap is part of the board. Forgetting it is how decks end one board short.

The 5.75 rule

A 5.5-inch board plus its 0.25-inch gap covers 5.75 inches per row - count the gap or buy the extra trip.

The frame truth

Joists at 16 inches, never 24 for 5/4 boards. Two screws per crossing, 75 to the pound. Decks fail at the ledger flashing, not the boards.

The wet-wood truth

Pressure-treated arrives wet. Stack it with spacers and wait a season before stain - staining wet PT peels by the second summer.
