A 5.5-inch board plus a 0.25-inch gap covers 5.75 inches per row - the gap is part of the board, and forgetting it is how decks end one board short.

The frame outlives the surface

Decks fail at the ledger flashing, not the boards. Joists go 16 inches on center, never 24 for 5/4 boards, two screws at every crossing - about 75 screws to the pound. Composite costs double up front and pays it back in unstained weekends, but it rides on the same honest frame. And pressure-treated lumber arrives wet: stack it with spacers and wait a season before stain, or it peels by the second summer.
